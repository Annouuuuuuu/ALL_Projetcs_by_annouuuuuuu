Instructions

1. Prerequisite Installation
Before running the program, make sure you have SWI-Prolog installed on your system.

2. File Preparation
• Place the plagiarism detector program in a directory of your choice.
• Add the Prolog files you wish to compare to this same directory.

3. Running the Detector
• Launch the logical plagiarism detector by double-clicking on its file.
• In the SWI-Prolog interface, enter the following query:

detecter_plagiat_complet('programme1.pl', 'programme2.pl'). 

4. Generated Results
The program will produce a report containing three distinct indicators:
• Signature Percentage: Measures the syntactic similarity between knowledge bases (facts).
• Normalized Clause Percentage: Assesses the logical similarities between the defined rules and their structure in the different programs.
• Overall Similarity Percentage: Represents the average of the two previous percentages.
